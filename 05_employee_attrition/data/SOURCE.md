# Dataset provenance

U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (2026). Quits rate 3.5 percent in accommodation and food services, August 2026. The Economics Daily.

Source: https://www.bls.gov/opub/ted/2026/quits-rate-3-5-percent-in-accommodation-and-food-services-august-2026.htm

Accessed: 2026-11-03.

raw.csv is a manually transcribed extract of the table of seasonally adjusted quits rates for August 2025 and 2026, including the all-industry total. Values are as published on October 13, 2026; August 2026 was preliminary. The BLS site blocked automated download; the table was accessed through the web reader.
